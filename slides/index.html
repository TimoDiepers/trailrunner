1 · the model

A cement plant is a few lines of Python.

trailrunner/models/cement.py · abridged
class CementPlant(Model):
    produces = [CEMENT]                     # …/BONSAI2025.1/fi_37440
    coverage = Coverage(time_range=(2026, 2050))

    def apply(self, demand):
        row = self.params.at(location=demand.flow.location, time=demand.flow.time)
        penalty = moisture_penalty(row["moisture"], row["temperature"])
        clinker = row["clinker_factor"] * demand.amount
        fuel = row["fuel_demand"] * clinker * penalty

        return Result(
            production=[Exchange(flow=demand.flow, amount=demand.amount, ...)],
            technosphere=[Demand(flow=here(NATURAL_GAS), amount=fuel, ...),
                          Demand(flow=here(LIME), ...), # limestone, power, kilns
            ],
            biosphere=[Exchange(flow=here(CO2_FOSSIL), amount=CLINKER_CALCINATION_CO2 * clinker, ...),
                       Exchange(flow=here(CO2_FOSSIL), amount=GAS_CO2_PER_MJ * fuel, ...)],
        )
Parameters per place & year — a trailpack parquet, units and IRIs embedded.
Physics — damp, cold air costs fuel.
Needs become demands — back on the queue, someone else answers.
Two CO₂ flows, not one — rock and flame, kept apart.

Conditions are named by IRI (QUDT), so a supplier only matches if it means the same pressure in the same unit. Gas is throttled at the burner, not boosted.

why we want this
  • The supply chain assembles itself. Vocabulary, not wiring.
  • A process reacts to its demand. Place, year, conditions.
  • Every concession is written down. Gaps are visible.
  • Inventories are time-explicit by construction.
