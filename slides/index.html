05 orchestration

The orchestrator is a single loop

while queue: Location and year travel with each flow. The loop never handles them explicitly.
06 resolution

Fallbacks are explicit and recorded

1
exact match
A model covers the concept, location, year and conditions.
2
relaxed demand
Broader concept, nearby location or year, or a tolerated condition.
3
background dataset
Taken from a background pack and tagged incomplete.
×
cutoff
Recorded with a reason at the node where it occurred.

Every fallback is stored on the node that used it, together with what was asked and what answered.

07 consequences

Properties that follow from the design

time
Every flow carries a year. The inventory is a time series, so dynamic characterization needs no extra model.
location
Gas used in Denmark is extracted and transported in Norway, and its burdens are computed there.
measurement
A metered plant is just another model. The demanded year decides which one answers.
provenance
The full run is written to one parquet file. Two studies can be compared with a single read.

08 the model

The cement plant model

trailrunner/models/cement.py (abridged)
class CementPlant(Model):
    produces = [CEMENT]                     # …/BONSAI2025.1/fi_37440
    coverage = Coverage(time_range=(2026, 2050))

    def apply(self, demand):
        row = self.params.at(location=demand.flow.location, time=demand.flow.time)  # 1
        penalty = moisture_penalty(row["moisture"], row["temperature"])  # 2
        clinker = row["clinker_factor"] * demand.amount
        fuel = row["fuel_demand"] * clinker * penalty

        return Result(
            production=[Exchange(flow=demand.flow, amount=demand.amount, ...)],
            technosphere=[Demand(flow=here(NATURAL_GAS), amount=fuel, ...),      # 3
                          Demand(flow=here(LIME), ...), # limestone, power, kilns
            ],
            biosphere=[Exchange(flow=here(CO2_FOSSIL), amount=CLINKER_CALCINATION_CO2 * clinker, ...),
                       Exchange(flow=here(CO2_FOSSIL), amount=GAS_CO2_PER_MJ * fuel, ...)],  # 4
        )
1Parameters by location and year, read from a trailpack parquet with units and IRIs embedded.
2Fuel demand depends on ambient moisture and temperature.
3Inputs are returned as new demands and go back on the queue.
4Calcination and combustion CO₂ are separate exchanges.

Conditions are identified by QUDT IRIs, so a supplier only matches the same quantity in the same unit. Gas can be throttled at the burner but not boosted, so lower pressure is never accepted.
